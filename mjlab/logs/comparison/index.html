Training the G1 to walk: PPO vs evolutionary methods

Every checkpoint below was replayed by eval_runs.py with the same seeds and no exploration noise. Return and survival come from 1,024 random-command 20 s episodes of the training task; course falls and tracking error from 64 robots on the fixed 20 s course. Color = algorithm, dashed = SGD variant, gray = PPO + Adam on the big network (the reference).

Episode return

Higher is better. Roughly 100 is a policy that walks to command for the full 20 s.

Survival

Fraction of the 1,024 robots that never fell in 20 s.

Course: velocity tracking error

m/s between commanded and actual velocity while upright. Lower is better; a robot standing still scores ~0.5.

ES in-training curves

Return of each ES run's mean policy during training (every generation, on its own training horizon, so not on the same scale as the evaluation above).

Final results

Last checkpoint of each run. "Time to 50" is wall-clock minutes until evaluation return first reached 50.
